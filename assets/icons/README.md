# Icons

SURF pictograms as black PNGs (about 252×242px), 24 per theme in seven folders: `collaboration/`, `data/`, `learning/`, `network/`, `research/`, `security/`, `strategy/`, numbered `<theme>-01.png` to `<theme>-24.png`. Their ink is black and cannot be recoloured through `<img>`; place them on white or light grounds.
